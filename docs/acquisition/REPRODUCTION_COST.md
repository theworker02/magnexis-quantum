## Why acquisition beats greenfield

Reproducing `magnexis-quantum` means rediscovering domain constraints, re-implementing architecture and edge cases, rebuilding tests/CI/packaging, and rewriting diligence materials already present in this repository. Buying the transferable IP is the faster path to a production-ready starting point.

## Product thesis

A lightweight, server-rendered engineering document portal for an independent research program: email-free accounts, access applications, collection-level approvals, protected research documents, and real classical simulations. The included physical-system engineering is a confidential proposal.
